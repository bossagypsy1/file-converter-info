Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

This application ("File Converter Tools") is a personal automation project built and used by a single individual (the "owner"). It is not offered as a public service and is not intended for use by anyone other than its owner.

What data is accessed

The application connects to the owner's own Google Drive account via Google OAuth, using the drive scope, to:

  • Read and download video and Ableton Live project files the owner has stored there
  • Write converted video/audio files, AI-separated audio stems, and patched project files back into the same Drive account
  • Rename, move, and organize files within that same Drive account

No Google Drive data belonging to anyone other than the owner is ever accessed, because the owner is the only person who authorizes or uses this application.

Third-party processing

When the owner chooses to run AI vocal/instrumental stem separation on a piece of audio, that audio file is uploaded directly to the MVSEP API for processing, and the resulting separated audio files are downloaded back and stored in the owner's own Google Drive. No other data is shared with MVSEP, and audio is never published to MVSEP's public demo page.

No other third-party services receive any of this data. There is no advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind in this application.

Data retention

This application does not operate its own database or server-side storage. All data it reads or writes lives entirely in the owner's own Google Drive, under the owner's own control, and can be deleted by the owner at any time through Google Drive directly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the contact email listed on this project's Google Cloud OAuth consent configuration.
